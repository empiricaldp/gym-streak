## The short version
- CREW stores what you need for your streak and your crew. Nothing more.
- We don't sell your data. We don't show ads.
- You choose who sees you: Public, or Private (only people you approve).
- You can delete your account and everything in it from Settings, at any time.

## What we collect
| What | Why |
|---|---|
| Email address and password (the password is stored scrambled, so we never see it) | To sign you in |
| Name, plate colour, split (your gym days) | Your profile |
| Gym days you log, streak freezes, workouts (exercises, sets, reps, weights, timers), plans | Your streak, your history, what your crew sees |
| Optional: goal, height, body weight, daily steps | Only if you add them; shown only where you choose |
| Buds, Circles, messages, nudges, reactions | The social parts of CREW |
| Blocks and reports you make | To keep people safe |
| Phone notification token (if you turn notifications on) | To send you notifications |
| Basic technical logs (when a notification was sent) | To stop duplicates and fix bugs |

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising IDs.

## Who can see what
- **Public account:** anyone signed in to CREW can see your profile, streak and gym days, and can spot you straight away.
- **Private account:** new people send a bud request you approve. People already spotting you keep seeing you until you remove them.
- **Circles:** people in a Circle with you can see your streak, week and split (unless you turn on "Hide my split").
- **Messages:** only the people in that chat.
- **Blocked people** can't see or contact you, and aren't told they're blocked.
- **Reports** are private. The person you report isn't told who reported them.

## Where it's stored
CREW uses Supabase (database and sign-in) [region: check in the Supabase dashboard → Project settings]. The app is hosted on GitHub Pages. Notifications go through Apple's and Google's push services. These companies store data for us and aren't allowed to use it for anything else.

## How long we keep it
- As long as your account exists.
- When you delete your account, your data is deleted straight away. The database provider's backups roll over within [X days, check the Supabase plan].
- If you undo a logged gym day, a copy of that day's workout is kept for 30 days in case it needs restoring, then deleted.

## Your choices and rights
- Change what you share: Settings → Privacy & safety.
- Delete your account: Settings → Account → Delete my account.
- Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it: email [support email].
- If you're unhappy with how we handled your data, contact us first. In Australia you can also complain to the OAIC (oaic.gov.au).

## Age
CREW is for people aged **[13 / 16 / 18]** and over. If we learn that an account belongs to someone younger, we'll delete it.

## Changes
If we change this policy in a way that matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
